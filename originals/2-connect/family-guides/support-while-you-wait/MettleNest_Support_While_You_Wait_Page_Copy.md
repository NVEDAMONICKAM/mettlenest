## The gap nobody prepares you for

You've noticed something. Maybe it's the school raising concerns, maybe it's your own gut. You've started the process — a referral, a wait list, a name on a spreadsheet somewhere.

And then: nothing, for a long time.

- Autism and ADHD assessment waitlists across Australia have been reported at up to **two years**
- Public system assessments can take **3.5 years** to complete in some regions
- Parents report an average of **12 months and eight appointments** before a diagnosis is reached
- Even after diagnosis, NDIS plan changes carry median waits of around **42 days** — sometimes well over 100

That's before therapy even starts. There's currently very little structured support offered to families during this entire window — which means most people are navigating the hardest, most uncertain stretch completely on their own.

## You don't need a diagnosis to start

This is the part most services don't say out loud: **you can access real support right now, before any assessment is complete.**

It's how the NDIS's own Early Childhood Early Intervention (ECEI) pathway already works — available to any child under 6 with a developmental delay, or under 9 with disability, without a diagnosis as a prerequisite. MettleNest works the same way. You don't need a letter from a specialist to start using the BRIDGE Method at home.

If you've been holding off reaching out because you're "waiting to know for sure" — that's exactly the gap this is built for.

## What support looks like during the wait

**BRIDGE Basics for Parents**
A self-paced course teaching the BRIDGE Method — the same practical, function-based approach to behaviour that clinics teach in their own short-wait parent training programs, just not badged as "waitlist support." Start using it the same week you sign up.

**BRIDGE Coaching**
One-to-one sessions for your specific situation, when a course alone isn't enough. No diagnosis required to book.

**The BRIDGE Behaviour Plan**
A working document you build together — behaviour, pattern, shared goal, strategies, language, and a review process — so you have something concrete in hand, not just advice to remember.

## FAQ

**Do I need a diagnosis to work with MettleNest?**
No. Many of the families we work with are still on a waitlist, partway through assessment, or have chosen not to pursue formal diagnosis at all. The BRIDGE Method doesn't require one.

**Is this a replacement for ABA therapy or NDIS-funded supports?**
No. MettleNest is designed to sit alongside those services, or to fill the gap before they start. If you're already receiving therapy, BRIDGE gives you a consistent way to reinforce it at home.

**What if we get a diagnosis later — do we need to start over?**
No. Everything you build with BRIDGE (your behaviour plan, your language, your strategies) carries forward. A diagnosis may add new information, but it doesn't undo the work you've already done.
