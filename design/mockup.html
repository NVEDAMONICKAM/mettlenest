About us

MettleNest's BRIDGE Method gives parents and teachers a shared language for understanding and responding to behaviour, so a child gets the same steady support at home and at school.

Why MettleNest exists

Behaviour support has always been a divided experience. Parents get parenting advice. Teachers get classroom management training. Meanwhile, the child at the centre hears two different sets of instructions, two different reactions to the same meltdown, and two different definitions of "doing well."

MettleNest exists to close that gap.

We believe behaviour is communication, not a problem to be managed away, and that the adults around a child do their best work when they're working from the same playbook. Not identical settings. Not identical rules. The same understanding of why the behaviour is happening, and the same approach to responding to it.

MettleNest exists to make behaviour support practical, respectful and within reach, so parents and educators can meet hard moments with confidence, connection and a clearer path forward.

Our approach

  • Behaviour is communication. We start by understanding it, not suppressing it.
  • Support should be humane and practical. No jargon-heavy plans that only work on paper.
  • Teachers are part of the support network, not a separate audience.
